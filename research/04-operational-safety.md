## Gaps and limitations found

**Legal**: Strong coverage for US/EU/China judgment- and clause-level classification (LEDGAR, CUAD, CaseHOLD, ECtHR, CAIL2018), but no real, licensed, non-synthetic dataset found for insurance-adjacent legal tasks (claims-denial-appeal classification) — public "legal outcome" work clusters around court judgments, not administrative/insurance tribunal decisions.

**Security/cyber**: Well-covered and well-licensed for intrusion-detection/malware (CIC-IDS2017, UNSW-NB15, BODMAS, SOREL-20M). Weak point: **access-control decision data** — real ABAC/RBAC grant/deny corpora are almost nonexistent publicly; the 2013 Amazon Employee Access Challenge is close to the only public one, and its license is Kaggle-competition terms rather than clean open. A genuine gap worth noting.

**Content moderation**: Generic-toxicity data (Jigsaw) abundant and well-licensed; datasets tied to *named platform policy categories* rather than generic toxicity are rarer — HateModerate is the one solid find, license unverified, needs a maintainer check.

**Medical**: Real clinical-triage data with a real severity scale exists (MIMIC-IV-ED/MIETIC, ESI 1-5) but behind PhysioNet's credentialed-access DUA — meaningful adoption friction worth flagging rather than silently including a dataset most users can't pull. CheXpert's finding-level labels are a good structural match for a null-aware primitive but gated behind non-commercial research-use agreement.

**HR/hiring**: Flagged most cautiously. Public resume-classification datasets are technically easy to transform but nearly all scraped from a single job-board source with no demographic auditing — risk of encoding proxy bias. No real, bias-audited hiring-decision dataset found that would justify inclusion without a fairness caveat.

**Deliberately not pushed further**: real hospital emergency-triage or diagnosis-adjacent datasets beyond MIMIC/CheXpert were visible in search but sit behind PHI-restricted credentialed access or are explicitly diagnosis-prediction rather than triage-routing — diagnosis-prediction sets were excluded even with a caveat, since a `choice`-typed diagnosis classifier is closer to practicing medicine than to the triage/routing use case ekVachan targets, and no caveat fully neutralizes that for a public training corpus.
